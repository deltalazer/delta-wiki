# deltalazer

**deltalazer** is a community-driven fork of osu!lazer that introduces **section gimmicks and hitobject gimmicks**: mapper-defined rules that can change gameplay behaviour throughout a beatmap. HP mechanics, difficulty settings and even mods can change from section to section.

- [Documentation](/wiki/Delta/Documentation): how to use section gimmicks, hitobject gimmicks and everything else that makes deltalazer unique
- [FAQ](/wiki/Delta/FAQ): answers to common questions about deltalazer and section gimmicks
- [Team](/wiki/Delta/Team): the people behind deltalazer
